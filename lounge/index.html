ABOUT

We're Passionate About Delivering Growth Services

PITSTOP21 is an innovative venture with a vision to offer luxurious and hygienic facilities that include restrooms, a homemade dining facility and a fully digitalized Banquet hall which is located en route to the famous Athirappilly waterfalls.

In addition, it also showcases various traditional products of tribal communities of Wayanad and Athirappilly. PITSTOP21 is the perfect stopover point for you to refresh your body and mind.

Banquet Hall

We offer a fully Air conditioned, digitalized hall with a seating capacity of 100 persons equipped with 65” Smart screen, Wireless internet connectivity, professional JBL sound system, and elegant stage setting.

Dining Hall

Our dining facility offers a unique experience to enjoy the authentic ‘Nadan’ (local) taste of Kerala and exotic flavours as well, which is hygienic, healthy and homely. The food will primarily be served to order via our website, based on our seasonal menu.

We offer food sourced from experienced homemakers, which will be authenticated by an HACCP-certified officer. We provide hygienic, healthy and homely food which can be pre-booked through our website.

Comfort Station

A premium comfort station with world class facilities maintaining the highest standards of hygiene. The facility is equipped with Western/Indian toilets, Urinals, Shower rooms fitted with Rain-shower options, Nursing rooms, Sanitary napkin dispenser and disposal, Changing rooms etc.

Secured Parking & shuttle services

To make your trip enjoyable & hassle free, we offer you ample parking facilities with shuttle services to Athirappilly/ Charppa waterfall locations.

Cloak room services

To secure your valuables you can avail our locker facility making your trip tension-free.

Space for Outdoor/Photo shoot events

PITSTOP21 is an ideal location for outdoor events like Wedding functions, Meetings, Cultural programs etc and for Photo shoots with its natural surroundings. We offer Decorative services as desired on demand to meet your requirements. To avail this space for any of your events contact us directly!

Workstation

Take a break from your desk and get work done enjoying the greenery of Athirapilly. We provide high speed internet connectivity with backup power, timely refreshments and access to all our amenities to make your time more comfortable.
